Bulleted list, with a secondary level

  • First top-level point, in full sentence form, describing something at a glance.
  • Second top-level point.
    • A nested, secondary point — subordinate to the item above.
    • Another nested point, shown with a hollow marker and muted color to signal it's one level down.
  • Third top-level point, back at the primary level.

Numbered list, with a secondary level

  1. First step in a sequence.
  2. Second step, which itself breaks into sub-steps.
    1. Sub-step one, shown lettered to distinguish it from the top level.
    2. Sub-step two.
  3. Third and final step.
